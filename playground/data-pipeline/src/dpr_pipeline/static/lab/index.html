Review lab

Three ways to look over applications quickly and approve them. They read from the ODP API and show sample data when it is not running. Add ?council=barnet to pick a council.

  • tb

    Tim-ber

    One application at a time. Drag right to approve, left to flag, up to see it later.

  • pt

    plannertok

    A full screen feed. Scroll to the next one, double tap to approve.

  • ip

    InstaPlan

    A photo style feed. Double tap the plan to approve, open the comments to read the record.

The admin has the tables and the bronze, silver and gold layers.
